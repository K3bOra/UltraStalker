FROM THE RECEIVER · من الرسيفر Load receiver backup

On the receiver: Settings › Storage & Maintenance › Backup & Restore › Create portable backup. Copy the file ultrastalker-backup-….zip from /media/hdd/UltraStalker/Backup and choose it here. It replaces the servers and Favorites saved on this site.

TO THE RECEIVER · للرسيفر Save backup for the receiver

Downloads ultrastalker-backup-….zip with the servers and Favorites from this site. Put it in /media/hdd/UltraStalker/Backup, then on the receiver open Backup & Restore › Restore backup.
